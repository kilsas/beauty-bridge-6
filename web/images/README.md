# Product photos

Put one photo per product here, named by product id: `P001.jpg`, `P001.png` or `P001.webp`
(square, at least 600x600, under ~150 KB each). Register each in `data/images.csv` with its
credit, license and source URL. `scripts/build_site.py` embeds them into the site; products
without a photo keep the drawn package illustration.

Use only photos you are allowed to publish: brand press kits or media centers (check
their terms), images supplied through an affiliate or partner program together with its
product feed, or photos you take yourself. Retailer and brand product photos are
copyrighted and are not free to reuse just because they are online.
